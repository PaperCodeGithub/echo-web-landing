**Name:** Echo

**Company:** echosocialconnect



Echo is a social media app. Unlike other social media it connects people who are physically near you by using location proximity.

It shows users about 300m to 1.3km. It shows nearby users real time. If user shares there other socials in echo, other people can see them too.



**Features:**



1\. Echo Radar: See nearby people (0.5 km to 10 km)

2\. Targeted Sonar (Instant): Instant scan to find specific people with desired profession or hobbies.

3\. Targeted Sonar (Notify Me): Set notify me and hang around, echo send you notification whenever the person you want comes nearby

4\. Whisper - user can post anything but only people around 5km can see this.

5\. Whispers can be posted completely anonymous so collage gossip, confessions, can be posted freely.

6\. whisper supports images, videos, pdfs, Instagram / YouTube short reels, Audios (can use in app voice changer to maintain anonymity).

7\. Other features like message someone, chats, profile etc are available.





**Pro-Feature:**



1. See who visited your profile
2. Hide yourself from the map.
3. Broadcast a whisper ( When you drop whisper, people near 5km will receive notification )





**Usage:**



1\. Dating

2\. Suppose a singer wants to join a band, and a band manager wants a singer. If they are traveling on a same train, there could be a huge opportunity but unfortunately they

don't interact and opportunity faded away but with echo it's solvable. echo sends you notification - "The person your looking for is near by.."

3\. Suppose your friend collapsed on the road. Ambulance can be late but what if there's a doctor near by ?

4\. Can be local news reporter by posting whispers everyday
